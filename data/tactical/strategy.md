# Tactical Strategy

Status: personalized initial plan, updated 2026-05-03.

## Operating Rules

- Directives from `data/strategic/directives.yaml` override preferences.
- No measured data means `unknown`, not guessing.
- Sleep beats training: if sleep is under 6 hours, use rest, walking, or Zone 1.
- Weekly averages matter more than one bad day.

## 90-Day Focus

- Primary aim: recomposition, body shape/abs, energy, and improved VO2max without aggressive cutting.
- Priority order: energy, health, productivity, body composition.
- Target date: 2026-08-01.
- Body fat percentage, steps average, strength baselines, VO2max, HRV, RHR, and biomarkers are unknown until measured.

## Nutrition

- BMR: 1669 kcal/day by Mifflin-St Jeor.
- TDEE estimate: 2250-2420 kcal/day using light/moderate activity factor 1.35-1.45 until 8-12k steps/day and 4-5 weekly training sessions are confirmed.
- Starting calories: 2450 kcal/day for 14 days.
- Macros: protein 160 g/day, fat 75 g/day, carbs 285 g/day.
- Protein: about 2.2 g/kg/day to support recomposition and training adaptation.
- Fiber: at least 25 g/day.
- Protein first at each meal; use 2-3 meals/day.
- No aggressive cut: keep carbs high enough to preserve training quality and cognitive energy.
- Saturated fat, added sugar, omega-3: follow `directives.yaml` when biomarkers are available.

## 14-Day Control Rule

- Weigh in every morning after bathroom, before food.
- Log sleep every day.
- Measure waist 2 times per week.
- If weight drops faster than 0.5 kg/week and energy or strength drops: add 100 kcal/day.
- If weight is stable, waist decreases, and strength is normal: keep 2450 kcal/day.
- If weight increases and waist increases: subtract 100 kcal/day.
- Review after 14 days.

## Training

- Strength: 4 gym sessions/week with hypertrophy emphasis if recovery allows.
- Zone 2: build toward 150 min/week.
- VO2max intervals: 1 session/week after 4 weeks of consistent Zone 2 and stable recovery.
- Progression: double progression, first reps, then load.
- Hammam/sauna: use as recovery after easy days or rest days, not as a replacement for sleep.

## Sleep and Recovery

- Target sleep window: 23:00-07:00.
- Target sleep: 8 hours.
- Keep bedtime and wake time within +/- 30 minutes.
- First caffeine: at least 90 minutes after waking.
- Last caffeine: at least 10 hours before sleep.
- NSDR: at least 3 sessions/week.

## Weekly Review

Track:
- weight trend
- waist trend
- calories and protein adherence
- strength sessions completed
- Zone 2 minutes
- sleep hours and regularity
- recovery notes
